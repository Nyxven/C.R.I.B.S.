Cribsy, inside the smart bear

Cribsy rides in your child's bear and doubles as their keychain. C.R.I.B.S. opens the location network you paired Cribsy with, so you can see where it is and make it ring.

Which network is Cribsy paired with?

Cribsy connects to one network at a time, so pick the one you used during setup.

Locate Cribsy About Cribsy and setup
C.R.I.B.S. does not read the tag's location itself. It opens Apple or Google's own app, which keeps location data private. Bluetooth trackers depend on nearby phones, so they can miss a spot with few people around. Never rely on Cribsy as the only safety measure for a child.

Privacy Policy

C.R.I.B.S. was developed as part of a Senior High School STEM research study on parenting guidance for children with Autism Spectrum Disorder.

Information you enter — your name, your child's profile, which network Cribsy is paired with, and messages you post — stays on this device only, for this demo. It is never uploaded, sold, or shared with third parties.

Content in the Autism Information, Communication, Behavior, Learning, and FAQ modules is intended as general guidance only. It does not replace diagnosis, therapy, or advice from a licensed healthcare professional, therapist, or special education specialist.

The Professional Support Directory lists sample listings for demonstration. Always verify a provider's credentials directly before booking care.
